Method notes

  • Publication statistics use every paper with a Group 6 affiliation, including students' papers. Faculty statistics and faculty networks use listed faculty only.
  • Large author lists: OpenAlex search results list at most 100 authors per paper. For the 310 papers with 100+ authors the full author list was fetched, which restored 1,691 hidden Group 6 author entries (mostly ALICE at CERN and Belle II).
  • Co-authorship network: excludes papers with 100+ authors. Being one of 1,000 consortium authors is not treated as a collaboration link.
  • Private vs government: classified by hand for the top 60 citing institutions (deemed / private universities vs central, state and government bodies).
  • Citations are OpenAlex counts at collection (Oct 2026). Google Scholar counts are typically higher.
